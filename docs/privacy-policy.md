# Stepkin privacy policy

Effective 9 September 2026.

Stepkin is a virtual pet for Apple Watch made by Cliftonia. This policy
explains what the app reads and where it goes. The short version: nothing
leaves your watch.

## What the app reads

- **Step count.** With your permission, Stepkin reads your daily step total
  from the Health app. The number decides when your creature evolves. It is
  read on the watch, used on the watch, and stored only in the game's own
  save on the watch.
- **Location.** With your permission, Stepkin reads your current location
  once every thirty minutes at most, only while the app is open, to ask
  Apple's weather service for the local conditions. Apple's handling of
  that request is covered by Apple's own privacy policy. Stepkin does not
  store your location.
- **Notifications.** With your permission, Stepkin schedules local
  reminders on the watch when your creature needs care. They are created
  and delivered on the device.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
- It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.
- It uses no third-party software.
- It makes no network request except the weather request to Apple.

## Where the save lives

Your creature's state is stored on the watch, in the app's own container,
and in a shared container read by the watch-face complication. Deleting the
app deletes both.

## Children

Stepkin does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children, because it collects no information at all.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address
with a new effective date.
